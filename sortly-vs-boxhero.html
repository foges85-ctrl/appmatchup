Sortly vs. BoxHero

Sortly is published by Sortly Inc. and has been on the App Store since 2012-06-05. BoxHero is published by BGPworks and has been available since 2019-05-23. Both are mobile inventory apps with barcode scanning, both start free at a hundred items, and both charge $288 for a year. What that $288 buys is where they separate. This comparison is built from what each developer publishes — App Store listings and both companies’ own pricing pages — with every claim sourced and anything unverifiable listed as such.

Everything below comes from published sources, checked on 2026-08-21 and linked in each section. Where the sources don’t answer a question, it’s listed under What we couldn’t verify rather than guessed at.

Sortly

A mobile inventory platform organised into folders and items, with barcode scanning, label generation and tiers priced per user licence.

BoxHero

An inventory app built around locations and transactions, priced per team rather than per seat.

The free plans are close enough to be interchangeable, and the paid plans cost the same for a year. The difference is what the money covers: BoxHero’s entry plan carries more items, more people and more locations at that price, while Sortly’s carries fewer of each but comes from a far more established app with a much longer track record.

Where each one fits

Sortly brings fourteen years on the App Store against BoxHero’s seven, a far larger body of ratings as the table above shows, folders and photos for physical items, and active jobs for connecting items to work orders. Against that, it requires iOS 18.5, its $288 tier carries half the items and fewer people than BoxHero’s, its pricing page does not state a transaction history, and its yearly rate is a first-year discount.

BoxHero brings twice the items, three members and three locations at the same annual price, unlimited transaction history on the paid plan, Excel import and export on the free plan, and an iOS 15.5 floor. Against that, it is a much newer and far less used app, and its free plan allows one location and thirty days of history.

What we couldn’t verify

  • Whether Sortly counts locations at all, which its pricing page does not mention as a limit
  • Whether either app’s in-app subscription price matches the price on its website, which App Store listings do not show
  • What BoxHero’s trial period length is, since its page states a free trial with no credit card required but names no duration
