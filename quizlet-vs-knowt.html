Quizlet vs. Knowt

Quizlet is published by Quizlet Inc and has been on the App Store since 2012-08-09. Knowt is published by Knowt Inc. and has been available since 2023-10-13. Both are free to download with paid tiers, and Knowt’s own listing describes importing flashcard sets from Quizlet. This comparison is built from what each developer publishes — App Store listings and Knowt’s own plans page — with every claim sourced and anything unverifiable listed as such.

Everything below comes from published sources, checked on 2026-08-09 and linked in each section. Where the sources don’t answer a question, it’s listed under What we couldn’t verify rather than guessed at.

Quizlet

A long-established study platform with a vast library of user-created sets, practice tests and homework solutions.

View on the App Store

Knowt

A newer study app built around AI-generated flashcards and notes, which its listing says can import sets from other platforms.

View on the App Store

Knowt is aimed at people leaving Quizlet: its listing describes importing Quizlet sets, and it advertises unlimited Learn mode on the free plan. If you want the largest library and the widest language support, Quizlet is still far bigger. If what sent you looking was hitting a paywall on the study mode you actually use, that is the gap Knowt is aiming at.

Where each one fits

Quizlet is the larger of the two by a wide margin — 17 languages against one, thirteen years on the App Store, and rating counts in the table above that are the clearest measure of the gap — and it carries expert-authored step-by-step homework solutions, which Knowt does not offer. Against that, its listing does not state what the free plan includes, and offline access to saved decks sits behind the paid tier. Quizlet vs. Brainscape runs into the same gap against an app that publishes what its free tier includes.

Knowt publishes its prices, gives unlimited rounds of Learn mode on the free plan, imports sets from other platforms including Quizlet, and generates flashcards and practice tests from notes, PDFs and lecture videos. It also runs on iOS 16.4 against Quizlet’s 17. Against that, it is English only, and it is far smaller than Quizlet by any measure in the table above.
