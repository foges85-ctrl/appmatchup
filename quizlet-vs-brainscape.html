Quizlet vs. Brainscape

Quizlet is published by Quizlet Inc and has been on the App Store since 2012-08-09. Brainscape is published by Bold Learning Solutions and has been available since 2011-07-15. Both are free to download with paid subscription tiers, and both build on spaced repetition. This comparison is built from what each developer publishes — App Store listings and Brainscape’s own pricing page — with every claim sourced and anything unverifiable listed as such.

Everything below comes from published sources, checked on 2026-08-08 and linked in each section. Where the sources don’t answer a question, it’s listed under What we couldn’t verify rather than guessed at.

Quizlet

A large general study platform built around user-created sets, with practice tests, adaptive question modes and homework solutions alongside flashcards.

Brainscape

A focused flashcard app built on confidence-rated spaced repetition, with an expert-certified library alongside user-created and community decks.

If you want one app that also generates practice tests, answers homework questions and carries a vast library of student-made sets, Quizlet is the broader platform. If you want flashcards done carefully — rating your own confidence on each card and letting the schedule follow from that — Brainscape is built around exactly that and very little else.

Where each one fits

Quizlet is the far larger of the two, and it does more than flashcards — practice tests, multiple choice, true/false and written question modes, and expert-authored step-by-step homework solutions, on top of a library of millions of sets created by students and teachers. It is listed in 17 languages against Brainscape’s one. Against that, its free tier’s limits are not published on the listing, which says only that AI features, offline access and ad removal sit behind the paid tier. And it is a far larger install than Brainscape, as the table above shows. The same unpublished free tier comes up in Quizlet vs. Knowt, where the other app does publish its limits.

Brainscape publishes what its free tier includes: unlimited flashcards you make or that are shared with you, plus 2,500 pre-loaded cards, with unlimited certified and community content on Pro at $7.99/month billed yearly. Its review schedule follows from the confidence rating you give each card, from 1 to 5, and its certified library covers MCAT, NCLEX, the bar exam, Series 7, GRE, AP courses, languages and certifications. Against that, it is English only, it does nothing beyond flashcards, and its listing does not state whether offline study is supported.
