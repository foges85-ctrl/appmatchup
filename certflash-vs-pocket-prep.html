CertFlash vs. Pocket Prep

CertFlash and Pocket Prep IT & Cybersecurity are both free to download, and both put most of their content behind a purchase. They are not the same kind of tool: CertFlash is a flashcard deck built on spaced repetition, and Pocket Prep is a practice-question bank with mock exams. This comparison is built from what each developer publishes — App Store listings and Pocket Prep’s own exam pages — with every claim sourced and anything unverifiable listed as such. CertFlash is published by Fogarty Holdings LLC, which also operates AppMatchup; see the disclosure below.

Everything below comes from published sources, checked on 2026-08-09 and linked in each section. Where the sources don’t answer a question, it’s listed under What we couldn’t verify rather than guessed at.

CertFlash

A CompTIA flashcard app built on spaced repetition, free on its first deck with a single purchase unlocking the rest.

Published by Fogarty Holdings LLC, which operates AppMatchup.

Pocket Prep

A practice-question bank with mock exams and analytics, covering IT and cybersecurity certifications from several vendors, sold as a subscription.

These are different tools more than they are rivals. If you want mock exams, performance analytics and coverage across many certification vendors, Pocket Prep is far broader and charges accordingly. If you want spaced-repetition drilling on CompTIA material with no subscription, that is the narrower thing CertFlash does.

Where each one fits

CertFlash is a single $5.99 unlock rather than a subscription, its free tier is a complete deck rather than a sample, it requires no account, it works fully offline after download, and it installs at 5.5 MB. The limits are plain. It covers seven CompTIA exams where Pocket Prep covers 26 across several vendors. It holds 1,050 cards against more than 25,000 practice questions. It offers no mock exams and no analytics. And it is a far newer and far less used app — the rating counts in the table above show the gap.

Pocket Prep is the broader tool by a wide margin: mock exams, performance analytics, peer score comparison, and 26 exams across CompTIA, Cisco, ISC2, ISACA, AWS and others, from a developer whose listing says professionals have relied on it since 2011. Against that, it is a recurring subscription at $124.99 a year even for a single exam, its free tier is 30 to 60 practice questions rather than a complete deck, and it requires iOS 18.0 where CertFlash runs on 17.0.
