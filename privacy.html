APP/MATCHUP

Privacy

AppMatchup is a static website. It has no accounts, no logins, no forms, no newsletter and no comments, so there is nothing to submit and nothing stored about you by this site directly. What follows covers the two third parties involved in serving and measuring it.

What this site collects directly

Nothing. There are no accounts and no sign-in. There are no forms, no newsletter, no comments, and no user-submitted content of any kind. There is nowhere on this site to enter information, so none is collected here.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is the only third-party script on the site. It sets cookies in your browser and reports which pages were viewed, an approximate location derived from your IP address, the type of device and browser used, and how visitors arrived at the site. Google truncates IP addresses.

Google’s handling of that data is covered by Google’s privacy policy. If you would rather not be measured at all, Google publishes a browser opt-out add-on.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, as part of delivering the pages. What GitHub does with those is covered by the GitHub general privacy statement.

Cookies

The only cookies set are the Google Analytics cookies described above. This site sets none of its own.

You can block or clear them in your browser settings without affecting the site. Nothing here requires a cookie to work — there is nothing to log in to and nothing to remember between visits.

Links to other sites

Comparison pages link out to App Store listings and to developers’ own pages. These are plain links, with no affiliate codes and no tracking parameters attached, and we receive nothing if you follow one.

Those sites are run by other people and have their own privacy policies, which govern what happens once you arrive.

Changes and contact

If the site’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to match, and the date below will change with it.
