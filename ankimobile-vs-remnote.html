AnkiMobile vs. RemNote

AnkiMobile is published by Anki Software LLC and has been on the App Store since 2010-05-27. RemNote is published by RemNote, LLC and has been available since 2023-01-22. AnkiMobile is a single paid purchase; RemNote is free to download with paid tiers. Both build on spaced repetition. This comparison is built from what each developer publishes — App Store listings and RemNote’s own pricing page — with every claim sourced and anything unverifiable listed as such.

Everything below comes from published sources, checked on 2026-08-08 and linked in each section. Where the sources don’t answer a question, it’s listed under What we couldn’t verify rather than guessed at.

AnkiMobile

The paid iOS companion to Anki, the free open-source desktop flashcard program, written by Anki’s own lead developer.

RemNote

An all-in-one notes and flashcard app where cards are made from your own notes, free to start with paid tiers for heavier use.

If you already run Anki on a computer and want your decks on your phone, AnkiMobile is the official companion and the purchase funds the desktop program. If you want your notes and your flashcards in one place, made from the same material, RemNote is built that way and costs nothing to start.

Where each one fits

AnkiMobile is $24.99 once, with no subscription, and its listing says the price is set as a computer application because sales fund development of both the desktop and mobile versions. It includes a free cloud synchronisation service that keeps card content and study progress in step across mobile and computer devices, and it is listed in 25 languages. Against that, there is no free tier on iOS, it does nothing beyond flashcards and study scheduling, and it has been updated less recently than RemNote, as the version dates in the table above show.

RemNote is free to start, and it keeps notes and flashcards in one app — documents and outlines, PDFs and backlinks, with cards generated from your own notes — plus annotated PDFs, image occlusion cards and handwritten documents. Its pricing is published: Pro at $8/month or $96 billed yearly, Pro with AI at $18/month or $216 billed yearly. Against that, the free plan caps annotated PDFs, image occlusion cards and handwritten documents at 3, 5 and 1 respectively, it is English only, and its listing does not mention sync.
