Corrections

If a comparison states something inaccurate about an app, say so. Include the page it is on and the claim you are pointing at, so it can be checked against the source it came from.

Corrections to factual claims are made on the page directly. Developers who want a detail about their own app corrected are explicitly welcome to write.

Suggestions

Suggestions for apps worth comparing are welcome. A suggestion is not a guarantee that a comparison gets written — each one takes a fair amount of reading, and only some pairs end up making a useful page.

We do not accept payment for placement or coverage, which is the same commitment made on the About page.
